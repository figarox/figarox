# Hi there, Maciej Rożnawski here 👋

## About me 😄
Hi, my name is Maciej Rożnawski. I graduated from the IT Technical School in Słupsk, passed the vocational exam, and obtained a certificate of professional qualification.

Why programming?

My passion for programming started in childhood, when I was around 9 years old. From an early age, I was inspired by creating my own things and by the possibility of turning ideas into something that actually works. When I was 11, I created my first 2D game, which sparked my interest in the world of programming even more.

Later, by choosing a technical school with an IT profile, I was able to continue developing this passion. The knowledge I gained during classes quickly became insufficient for me, so I enrolled in many online courses, which helped me expand my skills and gain new experience.

At first, I focused on frontend development, but over time I discovered that it was not enough and that I wanted to keep growing. I also started learning backend development. This made me realize that this is the direction I want to continue developing in, and the one I want to build my professional future around.

I worked for one year with the company that created the online store suprizo.pl, under a B2B contract.
